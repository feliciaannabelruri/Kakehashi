Optimization objective

maximize  Σ ( P(convert | customer, reward) × goal_value(reward) )
subject to  reward_cost ≤ partner_budget  ∧  reward ∈ customer_affinity_set

The four factors from the deck — customer preference × merchant objective × reward cost × conversion probability — are the terms above. The engine never shows a reward the customer has no affinity for, and never one the partner can’t fund.

Business Model

Four revenue streams. Network-effect defensibility.

Merchant starts free and pays only after seeing results. Rewards are funded entirely by digital partners — never by merchants.

01

¥2,000–5,000 /mo
